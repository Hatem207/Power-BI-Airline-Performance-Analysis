This Power BI dashboard provides an interactive analysis of airline and flight performance. It explores flight operations, airline performance, delays, cancellations, airports, routes, and overall flight activity to identify operational trends and performance patterns.

## Business Problem

Airlines need to monitor flight performance, delays, cancellations, airport activity, and route patterns to improve operational efficiency and customer experience. Analyzing flight data helps identify major delay factors, compare airline performance, monitor cancellation trends, and understand airport and route activity.

## Dashboard Features

### Executive Overview

* Monitor overall flight activity.
* Track on-time performance and cancellation rates.
* Analyze flight volume over time.
* Compare airline performance.
* Monitor average arrival delays.
* Analyze total flight distance.

### Airline Performance

* Compare airlines based on total flights.
* Analyze on-time performance across airlines.
* Compare average arrival delays.
* Analyze cancellation rates by airline.
* Identify different sources of airline-related delays.

### Delay Analysis

* Analyze average arrival and departure delays.
* Monitor overall delay rates.
* Compare delayed and on-time flights.
* Analyze total delay minutes.
* Identify primary delay reasons.
* Analyze delay patterns across days and months.
* Compare different delay categories such as:

  * Air System Delay
  * Airline Delay
  * Late Aircraft Delay
  * Weather Delay
  * Security Delay

### Cancellation Analysis

* Monitor total cancelled flights.
* Analyze cancellation rates.
* Compare cancellation performance across airlines.
* Analyze cancellation trends over time.
* Identify cancellation reasons.
* Compare cancellation reasons across different airlines.

### Airport & Route Analysis

* Analyze flight activity by origin airport.
* Analyze flight activity by destination airport.
* Compare airport activity by state.
* Analyze average departure delays by airport.
* Explore airport and route patterns.
* Analyze total flight distance.
* Visualize airport locations using geographic information.

### Flight Details

* Explore individual flight records.
* Analyze flights by airline.
* Filter flights by month.
* Filter flights by origin state.
* Filter flights by destination state.
* Analyze flights based on delay reasons.
* Review flight numbers, delays, distance, cancellations, and diversions.

## Key Insights

* Airline performance varies across on-time rates, average delays, and cancellation rates.
* Flight delays can be influenced by multiple operational and external factors.
* Late aircraft, airline operations, air system conditions, and weather are among the delay categories analyzed in the dashboard.
* Cancellation patterns differ across airlines and over time.
* Airport and route activity provides useful information for understanding flight volume and operational performance.
* Monitoring flight performance over time helps identify changes in delays, cancellations, and overall operational activity.
